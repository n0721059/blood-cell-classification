WBC FEATURE SET — ANNOTATION GUIDE  (v3 reduced, 32 features)
Based on: Tavakoli et al. (2021), Scientific Reports 11:20218
================================================================================

REGION DEFINITIONS
------------------
Ncl   Nucleus             : the segmented nuclear mask pixels
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm   : cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels (the cytoplasm ring)
Cvx   Convex Hull         : smallest convex polygon enclosing the whole cell
                            used as denominator to normalise for illumination variation

STATISTIC DEFINITIONS
---------------------
mean  : average value of the channel across all pixels in that region
std   : standard deviation of the channel across all pixels (measures texture / spread)

COLOUR CHANNEL DEFINITIONS
--------------------------
R     : Red channel (RGB) — raw red light intensity
G     : Green channel (RGB) — raw green light intensity; correlates strongly with L
B     : Blue channel (RGB) — raw blue light intensity; dominant in hematoxylin-stained nuclei
H     : Hue (HSV) — dominant colour angle on the colour wheel (0–360 deg)
S     : Saturation (HSV) — colour purity; 0 = grey, 1 = vivid colour
V     : Value / brightness (HSV) — maximum of R, G, B
L     : Lightness L* (CIE L*a*b*) — perceptual brightness fully decoupled from colour
A     : a* axis (CIE L*a*b*) — green-to-red opponent colour; negative = green, positive = red
Bstar : b* axis (CIE L*a*b*) — blue-to-yellow opponent colour; negative = blue, positive = yellow
Y     : Luma (YCbCr) — brightness approximation; near-identical to L (dropped in v3)
Cr    : Red-difference chroma (YCbCr) — how much redder than neutral grey
Cb    : Blue-difference chroma (YCbCr) — how much bluer than neutral grey (dropped in v3)

================================================================================
NCLCVX MEAN FEATURES  (nucleus brightness and colour, relative to convex hull)
================================================================================

Feature name: NclCvx_H_mean
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(H in convex hull)
H     Hue (HSV)  → dominant colour angle; distinguishes purple nucleus from pink cytoplasm
mean  Average across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The average hue of the nucleus, relative to the whole-cell average hue.
Biology: Useful for detecting cells where nucleus colour diverges strongly from cytoplasm
         (e.g. erythroblasts late stage where cytoplasm shifts pink while nucleus stays purple).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_L_mean
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(L in convex hull)
L     CIE L* lightness  → perceptual brightness, decoupled from colour
mean  Average across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The average brightness of the nucleus, relative to the whole-cell average brightness.
Biology: Expected to be < 1.0 for all cell types because the nucleus absorbs hematoxylin
         and stains dark purple-blue, making it darker than the surrounding cytoplasm and
         background. A value >= 1.0 indicates a likely segmentation or staining error.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_A_mean
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(A in convex hull)
A     CIE a* axis  → green-to-red opponent colour
mean  Average across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The average green-red colour tone of the nucleus, relative to the whole-cell average.
Biology: Typically near-neutral or slightly negative (blue-purple nuclei have low a*).
         A strongly positive value would indicate unexpected reddish nuclear staining.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_Bstar_mean
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(b* in convex hull)
Bstar CIE b* axis  → blue-to-yellow opponent colour; canonical blue-axis feature
mean  Average across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The average blue-yellow colour tone of the nucleus, relative to the whole-cell average.
Biology: Expected to be negative (more blue than yellow) for all nuclei due to hematoxylin staining.
         Basophil nuclei show the most strongly negative values. Retained in v3 as the canonical
         blue-axis representative; Cb_mean dropped (r=0.997 with Bstar_mean).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_Cr_mean
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(Cr in convex hull)
Cr    YCbCr red-difference  → how much redder than neutral grey
mean  Average across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The average redness of the nucleus, relative to the whole-cell average redness.
Biology: Normally low because nuclei stain purple-blue, not red. Elevated values could indicate
         background contamination or a cell type where nuclear staining has a warm tone.

================================================================================
NCLCVX STD FEATURES  (nucleus colour texture / internal heterogeneity)
================================================================================

Feature name: NclCvx_R_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(R in convex hull)
R     Red channel (RGB)  → raw red light intensity
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of red intensity within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average redness.
Biology: High values indicate heterogeneous red distribution inside the nucleus, which can
         reflect condensed chromatin patches or early degranulation artefacts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_B_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(B in convex hull)
B     Blue channel (RGB)  → raw blue light intensity
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of blue intensity within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average blueness.
Biology: Blue is the dominant staining channel for nuclei. High std indicates heterogeneous
         chromatin staining — dense heterochromatin patches appear darker blue than open euchromatin.
         Useful for distinguishing mature condensed nuclei (lymphocyte) from immature open nuclei (ig).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_H_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(H in convex hull)
H     Hue (HSV)  → dominant colour angle
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The colour variety within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell colour variety.
Biology: High values mean the nucleus contains pixels of multiple distinct hues, indicating
         complex chromatin texture or granular inclusions within the nuclear boundary.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_S_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(S in convex hull)
S     Saturation (HSV)  → colour purity
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of colour saturation within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell saturation.
Biology: High std means some areas of the nucleus are more vividly stained than others.
         Elevated in basophils where dense granules create vivid focal staining patches.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_L_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(L in convex hull)
L     CIE L* lightness  → perceptual brightness; canonical luminance texture feature
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of brightness within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average brightness.
Biology: High values indicate high internal contrast within the nucleus — condensed chromatin
         (dark) next to open chromatin (lighter) produces larger L* variation.
         Retained in v3 as canonical luminance texture; G_std and Y_std dropped (r > 0.994).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_A_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(A in convex hull)
A     CIE a* axis  → green-to-red opponent colour
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of green-red colour tone within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average.
Biology: High values mean some nuclear pixels are reddish while others are greenish — unusual
         and may indicate mixed staining artefact or boundary overlap with cytoplasm.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_Bstar_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(b* in convex hull)
Bstar CIE b* axis  → blue-to-yellow opponent colour; canonical blue-axis texture feature
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of blue-yellow colour tone within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average.
Biology: High values indicate uneven blue hematoxylin staining inside the nucleus — characteristic
         of heterochromatin patterns. Retained in v3 as canonical blue-axis texture; Cb_std dropped (r=0.997).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: NclCvx_Cr_std
Ncl   Nucleus  → pixels belonging to the segmented nuclear region
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(Cr in convex hull)
Cr    YCbCr red-difference  → how much redder than neutral grey
std   Standard deviation across all nucleus pixels
Plain language: The spread of redness within the nucleus, relative to whole-cell average redness.
Biology: High values indicate patchy warm-toned areas inside the nuclear boundary.
         Low values confirm a uniformly blue-purple nucleus as expected under Giemsa stain.

ROCCVX MEAN FEATURES  (cytoplasm ring colour, relative to convex hull)
================================================================================

Feature name: RocCvx_G_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(G in convex hull)
G     Green channel (RGB)  → raw green light intensity
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average green channel brightness of the cytoplasm, relative to whole-cell average.
Biology: Correlates with overall cytoplasm brightness. Retained in v3 for RocCvx means as the
         single RGB representative (G_mean not as redundant here as in NclCvx where L dominates).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_H_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(H in convex hull)
H     Hue (HSV)  → dominant colour angle on the colour wheel
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average dominant colour of the cytoplasm ring, relative to whole-cell average hue.
Biology: Distinguishes pink cytoplasm (neutrophil, H near 0/360) from blue-grey cytoplasm
         (monocyte, H near 200-240) and pale/near-neutral cytoplasm (lymphocyte).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_S_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(S in convex hull)
S     Saturation (HSV)  → colour purity; 0 = grey, 1 = vivid
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average colour vividness of the cytoplasm ring, relative to whole-cell saturation.
Biology: High in eosinophils (vivid orange granules) and basophils (dense purple granules).
         Low in lymphocytes (pale, nearly grey cytoplasm) and some neutrophils.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_L_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(L in convex hull)
L     CIE L* lightness  → perceptual brightness; canonical luminance feature
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average brightness of the cytoplasm ring, relative to the whole-cell average brightness.
Biology: Expected to be > 1.0 because cytoplasm is lighter than the nucleus and background combined.
         Confirms nucleus-cytoplasm brightness contrast when compared against NclCvx_L_mean (which is < 1.0).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_A_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(A in convex hull)
A     CIE a* axis  → green-to-red opponent colour
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average pink-to-green colour tone of the cytoplasm, relative to the whole-cell average.
Biology: High (positive) in eosinophils — orange-red granules push a* strongly positive.
         Negative or near-zero in monocytes and lymphocytes (blue-grey or pale cytoplasm).
         One of the most discriminative features for eosinophil identification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_Bstar_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(b* in convex hull)
Bstar CIE b* axis  → blue-to-yellow opponent colour; canonical blue-axis feature
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average blue-yellow colour tone of the cytoplasm ring, relative to whole-cell average.
Biology: Negative (blue) in basophils where granules spill blue stain into cytoplasm.
         Near-neutral or positive in eosinophils (orange-yellow cytoplasm).
         Retained as canonical blue-axis feature; RocCvx_Cb_mean dropped (r=0.985).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: RocCvx_Cr_mean
Roc   Ring of Cytoplasm  → cell mask pixels minus nucleus mask pixels
Cvx   Convex hull normalisation  → divided by mean(Cr in convex hull)
Cr    YCbCr red-difference  → how much redder than neutral grey
mean  Average across all cytoplasm ring pixels
Plain language: The average redness of the cytoplasm ring, relative to the whole-cell average redness.
Biology: The single most discriminative colour feature in the set. Distinctly elevated in
         eosinophils (bright orange-red granules). Low in lymphocytes, neutrophils, and monocytes
         (pale or blue-grey cytoplasm). Also slightly elevated in mature erythroblasts.

SHAPE FEATURES  (nucleus geometry — colour-independent)
================================================================================

Feature name: Circularity
Formula: P^2 / (4 * pi * A)   where P = nucleus perimeter, A = nucleus area
Range: >= 1.0  (equals 1.0 for a perfect circle, increases with irregularity)
Plain language: How far the nucleus departs from a perfect circle.
                A circle scores exactly 1.0. Any deviation increases the score without limit.
Biology: Low (1.0–1.3) in lymphocytes — round compact nucleus.
         High (1.5–3.0) in neutrophils — multi-lobed nucleus connected by thin strands.
         Intermediate in eosinophils (bi-lobed, ~1.2–2.5) and monocytes (kidney-shaped, ~1.1–1.6).
         Key check: lymphocyte mean < neutrophil mean. Reversal indicates segmentation error.
Reference: Tavakoli et al. (2021), Table 1.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: Convexity
Formula: P_hull / P   where P_hull = convex hull perimeter, P = nucleus perimeter
Range: (0, 1]  (equals 1.0 when nucleus has no concave indentations)
Plain language: How smooth and convex the nucleus boundary is.
                1.0 means the boundary never curves inward. Lower values mean deeper indentations.
Biology: High (0.85–1.0) in lymphocytes — smooth round boundary.
         Low (0.50–0.90) in neutrophils — lobulation creates large gaps between actual
         perimeter and convex hull perimeter.
         Key check: lymphocyte mean > neutrophil mean.
Reference: Hoffbrand & Moss, Essential Haematology 7th ed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Feature name: Solidity
Formula: A / A_hull   where A = nucleus area, A_hull = convex hull area
Range: (0, 1]  (equals 1.0 when nucleus fills its convex hull completely)
Plain language: How compactly the nucleus fills its own bounding convex shape.
                1.0 means no gaps or concavities. Lower values mean the nucleus leaves
                empty space inside its convex hull (inter-lobe gaps in segmented nuclei).
Biology: High (0.85–1.0) in lymphocytes — compact nucleus, no gaps.
         Low (0.45–0.88) in neutrophils — inter-lobe bridges create concave gaps that
         reduce nucleus area relative to its convex hull.
         Key check: lymphocyte mean > neutrophil mean.
Reference: Ross & Pawlina, Histology 8th ed., Chapter 13.
